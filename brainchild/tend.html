The Problem

Every writer who publishes consistently faces the same paradox: the more they create, the less they connect. Readers subscribe but never hear from you personally. Old friends go months without contact. The relationships do not die — they wilt.

The tools that exist to solve this were built for salespeople, not humans. CRMs track deals, not friendships. Nothing on the market helps a thoughtful person stay meaningfully connected.

How It Works

Listen

Tend connects to your email, fetches your real correspondence, filters out the noise, and routes every genuine message to the right person's folder. Your inbox is reorganized by relationship, not by date.

Know

Every person in your world gets a living profile — who they are, what matters to them, how you are connected, and what you last talked about. Tend remembers what you forget.

Reach

When it is time to connect, Tend surfaces the person who most needs to hear from you and drafts a message in your voice. Twenty message types cover every occasion. You review, refine, and send.

The Daily Practice

  • Import — Pull new messages (2 minutes)
  • Route — Messages find their people automatically
  • Compose — Draft one thoughtful message (5 minutes)
  • Reflect — Review your communication rhythm (3 minutes)
