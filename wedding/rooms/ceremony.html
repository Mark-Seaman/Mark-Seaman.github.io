The Ceremony

The ceremony at 5:00 on Monday, October 12, 2026, officiated by our friend Rick Olmstead. These are the vows we spoke to each other.

Mark's Vows

Kim, when I met you, I thought my life was mostly over. I believed the best years were behind me, and I spent my days trying to feel content with the good life I had already lived. Then you came, and everything changed.

With you I discovered that I was not done, and that I was not alone. Your life inspired me. The way you have poured yourself, year after year, into the lives of other people made me want to be a better man and to make a difference in the world again.

A year ago, before I knew your name, I wrote a description of the woman I hoped to marry. I decided I would wait until God brought her into my life. When I read those words again after meeting you, I was amazed at how clearly they describe you.

The unlikely way we met, and the many people and places we already shared, convinced me that something more than chance was at work. I will hold on to that in the hard seasons ahead. Only a few times in my life have I known with certainty that God was leading me. This is one of them.

So today I give you my life. I promise to build a home with you, to listen to you, and to put your needs beside my own. I promise to share both the joys and the sorrows that life will bring, and to keep choosing you in each of them.

I am not looking back today. I am beginning a new life with you, and I can hardly wait to see what God will do with the two of us together.

Kim's Vows

Mark, I can hardly believe I'm standing here with you today. Like most little girls, I dreamed about my wedding day. I just never imagined I'd be a first-time bride with gray hair and a Medicare card!

For so many decades, I wondered if there would ever be a man who truly loved and understood me, with whom I could share my heart and my life. I questioned God, often with tears and even a little yelling along the way. And then came you.

I love your brilliant mind, your quiet strength, and especially the way you see me. I've traveled a less traditional path in life, and I love that you delight in all the things that make me uniquely me.

With you, I can simply be myself. You bring me peace. I feel safe, known, cherished, and completely at home. You've awakened parts of my heart I had tucked away for so long, and reminded me that God still had beautiful things in store for my life.

But what I love most about you is the way you pursue God. Knowing that your heart is turned toward Him gives me the confidence to trust you with my heart and our future together.

Today, I promise to be your biggest cheerleader, to help you take life less seriously, and to embrace whatever adventures God has ahead for us. I promise to walk beside you as we pursue God together, and to keep choosing you and loving you through every season of our lives.

Mark, I've waited a very long time for you. And you were worth the wait.
