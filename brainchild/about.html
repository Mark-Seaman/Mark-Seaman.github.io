About Brainchild

The story behind the system.

Brainchild Publishing was born from a simple observation: the wisest people I know have never published a word.

They have decades of experience, hard-won insight, and a genuine desire to help others. But the publishing industry was not built for them. It was built for people with agents and marketing budgets. The self-publishing world was not built for them either. It was built for people with technical skills and entrepreneurial hustle.

I spent forty years as a software engineer and twenty years as a writer. When I lost my wife Stacie after 45 years of marriage, writing became my way of making sense of the world. I wrote about grief, about faith, about what it means to build a life worth living. The ideas came easily. Getting them published did not.

So I built the tools.

What started as a personal publishing pipeline grew into Forge — an AI-powered writing workshop that handles every mechanical stage between an idea and a Kindle book. The daily practice of writing a thousand words and publishing weekly became Kindling — a production process that guarantees a book every five months. The need to stay connected to readers and friends became Tend — a relationship system built on ten minutes of daily outreach.

Together these three products form the Brainchild ecosystem. I use them every day. They have produced over 600,000 words across 59 publications, daily Substack content across five venues, and a growing catalog of Kindle books.

Now I want to give other writers the same system.

What I Believe

Every meaningful piece of writing starts as an idea that matters to one person. That idea is your brainchild — born from your experience, shaped by your thinking, and waiting to reach the people who need it.

I believe that the world has enough content and not enough voice. I believe that one honest book changes more lives than a thousand social media posts. I believe that the people who most deserve to be published are the ones least likely to fight through the system to make it happen.

Brainchild exists to change that equation.

The Values

Voice over volume. Publish what matters, not what is easy.
Daily over heroic. Small, consistent effort beats occasional bursts of ambition.
Personal over scalable. Serve twenty readers well before chasing twenty thousand.
Dream over duty. Writing should feel like the thing you were meant to do.
