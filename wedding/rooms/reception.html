Linda's Toast

Linda McCallum, a friend of Kim's since 1989.

Today is a wonderful day, a day of hopes fulfilled. Of new beginnings and continued adventures.

I met Kim in 1989, just about this time of year. She decided to ditch the Bible study downstairs to hang out with me and the kids. We hit it off, and from then on we were always squeezing out time together. Through the years we managed to hang out, with Kim often and kindly coming my way. Once she even risked coming near my house when the girls had chicken pox. We sat out on the front lawn while the girls watched Robin Hood for the hundredth time.

Beyond that, we have had adventures looking for moose in the neighborhoods of Grant Lake, and a Mexican birthday getaway with the girls. Mark, be ready and buckle up, because life with Kim can be a wild ride. Don't believe her if she says she can't speak Spanish!

While we adventured together to relaxing spots, Kim did a lot of adventuring for work, to exciting places like Nicaragua, Morocco, Russia, and the Philippines. I love the stories she told me of haggling with a Nicaraguan judge to buy property to be part of a children's home. There were many other times she had to stand before international authorities for children. For love, Kim is fearless!

Kim has a steadfastness that I so respect. She has kept her faith and love for our Lord Jesus in some of the darkest times and the best of times, trusting in his love for her and that he had a plan for her next steps in life. And then in faith, she stepped out to build a successful business, and to meet a great man!

Kim loves making a home for family to be together. From roommates to children's homes around the world, family has been her heart. It gives me so much joy that you, Mark, are giving Kim a beautiful family to do life with.

I was reminded just the other day, as we were going to dinner at Mark's and had to arrive before sunset, of a beautiful moment. Kim wanted us to partake in the beauty of the sunset. It reminded me of the many times Kim has strived to create, or to highlight the importance of creating and enjoying, the beauty of God's creation. Fortunately, it led us to a cliff edge! Close, though!

Another beautiful aspect of Kim is her willingness to bring others into her process and be a team. And if there isn't a team, there will be. So many times I have seen Kim play a big part in developing a ministry or expanding her business.

I see you two having a wonderful, love-filled partnership, stepping into new ways to share your faith with others (that might be on a bus or at a timeshare meeting) and building up the body of Christ. Geez, you have already started! But beyond ministry, which will flow out no matter what, I see you traveling, discovering new places, and making friends around the world and in Colorado.

With Mark's ability to speak Greek and Kim's Spanish and Russian, imagine where they will go!

My friends, join me in raising a glass to toast this new marriage.
