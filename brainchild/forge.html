The Problem

Writing is easy. Publishing is hard. The distance between a good idea and a finished book is filled with dozens of mechanical tasks that have nothing to do with creativity. Most writers stall not because they run out of words but because they run out of process.

AI made the writing faster. It did not make the publishing simpler. You can produce five thousand words in an hour and still have no idea how to turn them into a book a reader can buy.

The Six-Stage Pipeline

1. Extract

Forge's Truth Extractor reads everything you have written on a topic and surfaces the structural argument hiding inside it. Not a summary — a skeleton. The book that already exists inside your scattered thinking.

2. Outline

The extracted truth becomes a 4x4 outline: four major sections, each with four subtopics, yielding sixteen to twenty articles. One document that defines the entire book.

3. Draft

Forge expands each outline node into a full article, matching your voice and following your structural intent. You review and revise. The AI handles the heavy lifting; you make the editorial decisions.

4. Transform

Three built-in operations reshape content to fit any format. Expand adds depth. Condense cuts length. Refine improves clarity.

5. Assemble

Articles become chapters. Chapters become a manuscript. The Book Factory produces EPUB and PDF. A cover is built from your metadata.

6. Publish

The finished book goes to Kindle. Promotional materials are generated automatically. Your subscribers are notified.
